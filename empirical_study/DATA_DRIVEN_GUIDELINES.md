# Data-Driven Guidelines for Population Adjustment Method Selection

Based on empirical analysis of 12 published HTA submissions

---

GUIDELINE 1: Select based on imbalance severity
  Based on: Analysis of 12 HTA cases

  • SMD < 0.39: STC viable (observed in practice)
  • SMD > 0.45: MAIC commonly used
  • SMD > 0.50: MAIC strongly preferred (n=7)

GUIDELINE 2: Consider sample size constraints
  • n < 200: Observed in 1 cases
    - Mean ESS ratio: 0.39
    - Risk of extreme weights
    - Consider STC if SMD < 0.4

GUIDELINE 3: Effective Sample Size thresholds
  Based on 7 MAIC applications:
  • Mean ESS ratio: 0.35
  • Minimum acceptable: 0.23 (observed in practice)
  • Below ESS ratio 0.20: Consider alternative method

GUIDELINE 4: Covariate dimensionality
  • ≥7 covariates: Observed in 4 cases
    - Mean ESS ratio: 0.32
    - Substantial efficiency loss with MAIC
    - Balance precision vs exact balance

GUIDELINE 5: Expected adjustment impact
  • Mean impact across all cases: 0.143
  • Cases with SMD > 0.5: Mean impact = 0.200
  • Cases with SMD < 0.3: Mean impact = 0.040
  → Adjustment more important when imbalance is severe
